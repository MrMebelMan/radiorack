Bendix/King KN 64 DME simulator

Practice trainer based on the Bendix/King Silver Crown Plus Pilot's Guide (KN 62A and KN 64 DMEs) and the KN 62/62A/64 Installation Manual (006-00144-0007 Rev 7). Standard version 066-1088-00. Mouse: drag a knob up/down or scroll over it; the outer ring and the center knob are separate. Click the center knob to pull it out or push it in. Drag a slide switch sideways, click where you want it, or scroll on it.

Procedures

Operation
  • Turn on the unit only after engine start-up, and turn the avionics off before engine shut-down.
  • The 3-position function switch determines both the information displayed and the channeling source.
Frequency (FREQ)
  1. Place the function switch on FREQ. The unit is channeled with its own two concentric frequency selection knobs.
  2. Smaller knob in: changes the 0.1 MHz digit (0.0, 0.1, 0.2 ...). Pulled out: adds 0.05 MHz and tunes in 0.1 MHz steps (0.05, 0.15, 0.25 ...). Pushing it in subtracts 0.05 MHz.
  3. The outer, larger knob changes the larger digits (1 MHz, 10 MHz).
  4. The unit displays distance and the selected frequency.
Groundspeed / Time-to-Station (GS/T)
  1. Move the function switch to GS/T. The unit holds the internally selected frequency and displays distance, groundspeed and time-to-station.
  2. Rotating the frequency selector has no effect (Frequency Hold): this prevents accidental rechanneling when the frequency is not displayed.
  3. After power-on (or a momentary power interruption) in GS/T, the unit shows dashes and stays in search: switch to FREQ or RMT to re-establish normal operation.
Remote (RMT)
  1. Place the function switch in RMT. The DME is channeled when you select your NAV frequency on the NAV receiver.
  2. Search time is usually about one second. When locked on a ground station, it displays distance, groundspeed and time-to-station.
  3. Prior to lock-on, dashes are displayed.
  4. You have two frequencies available at all times: one selected on the NAV receiver, one with the unit's own knobs.
Operational notes
  • The audio output identifies the DME ground station being received.
  • The distance is slant range, not ground distance; the difference is negligible when the range is three times the altitude or more.
  • Groundspeed (0 to 999 kt) is the rate of change of the slant range: accurate only tracking directly to or from the station. Time-to-station is accurate only tracking directly to the station.
  • On the ground the DME usually receives nothing (line of sight). After losing the signal it keeps the last reading for 11 to 15 seconds.
