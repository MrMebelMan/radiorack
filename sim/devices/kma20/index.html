King KMA 20 TSO audio control system simulator

Practice trainer based on the King brochure "Operating your KMA 20 Audio Control System" (006-8200-05) and the KMA 20/KR 21 Installation Manual (006-0044-02 Rev 2). Standard version 066-1024-03. Mouse: click the upper or lower half of a toggle switch to move it up or down (or scroll on it). Drag the microphone selector up/down or scroll on it. Hold the lower half of the MKR switch for TEST.

Procedures

Microphone transmission control
  • Rotary switch at the left: the microphone goes to the COMM 1 or COMM 2 transmitter, or to the external speaker (EXT: ramp hailer, cabin address or pilot-copilot intercom).
  • When you press the mike button, the output of all receivers to the speaker is muted, so there is no cockpit feedback.
  • On EXT, the receivers selected are heard on the EXT speaker until the microphone is keyed; then the microphone goes to the EXT speaker.
Audio reception control
  • Each toggle: up SPEAKER, middle OFF, down PHONE.
  • Pre-tune the receivers and listen to any of them: COMM 1, COMM 2, NAV 1, NAV 2, ADF, DME or Marker Beacon (MKR).
  • The pilot can listen to one radio on the speaker while the copilot listens to another on the headphones.
AUTO switch
  1. Put both COMM receiver toggle switches (COM 1, COM 2) on OFF.
  2. Set AUTO to SPEAKER or PHONE.
  3. You now hear the receiver of the COMM transmitter selected as you turn the microphone selector between COMM 1 and COMM 2.
Marker beacon
  • Outer marker: blue O lamp and 400 Hz dashes, two per second. Middle marker: amber M lamp and 1300 Hz dots and dashes. Airway / inner marker: white A lamp and 3000 Hz.
  • The MKR toggle routes the marker audio to the speaker or phones; the lamps work regardless.
  • Fly HI sensitivity until the marker is received (about one mile before the outer marker), then switch to LO for a more accurate indication of passage. The marker audio may then be turned off and the passage timed from the light only.
  • TEST (hold the switch down): lamp test.
