Becker AR6201 VHF transceiver simulator

Practice trainer based on the AR6201-(X0X) Operating Instructions (Issue 5, 2013) and Installation and Operation Manual (DV 14300.03 Issue 5), software 3.05 / 1.49. Mouse: drag a knob up/down or scroll over it to turn it; click the big knob to push it. Keys: click = short press, hold 2 s = long press. Hold the 8/25 mark to press MDE and STO together.

Procedures

4.2 Start-up / 4.3 receive & transmit
  • Turn the volume knob clockwise: WAIT with the software versions, then the mode used last.
  • FAILURE / PRESS ANY KEY: the start-up test found an error.
  • PTT: TX top-left. Most actions are blocked while transmitting; in Standard Mode the preset can still be changed. After 120 s the transceiver returns to receive (STUCK PTT); release PTT before transmitting again.
4.4 Frequency selection modes
  • Short MDE: Standard → Direct Tune → Channel → Standard.
  • Standard: active on top, preset below. Push the knob: MHz inverted; push again: 100 kHz; again: 25/8.33 kHz digits. Turn to change the preset. Short ↕ exchanges active and preset.
  • Direct Tune: the same on the active frequency (takes effect immediately); bottom line shows the battery voltage.
  • Channel: short push or one clockwise turn → channel number inverted, turn to select (tunes at once). Counter-clockwise turn → LAST channels. Short push or 5 s leaves.
4.4.4 Scan / 4.5 squelch / 4.7 spacing
  • Long ↕/SCN: Scan (watches active and preset; active has priority). Short MDE or long SCN ends it.
  • Short IC/SQL: squelch ON/OFF (SQL shown when on). With squelch off the arrow ► stays in front of the active frequency.
  • ► empty / half / full: poor / good / strong signal.
  • Hold the 8/25 mark (MDE + STO) 2 s: 8.33 ↔ 25 kHz spacing.
4.8 Store a channel and label
  1. STO: STO page, the proposed channel inverted (FREE / USED). Turn to choose 1–99.
  2. STO again: label edit (10 characters). Turn = character, push = next position.
  3. Short STO stores, long STO clears the label. No action for 7 s: back without storing.
  4. LAST1…9: a new active frequency kept 10 s is stored automatically.
4.12 Menus
  • Long IC/SQL: IC VOLUME (0–46) and IC VOX (−30…+10, above +10 = OFF). Short IC/SQL toggles the pages; long MDE or 5 s ends.
  • Long MDE: BRIGHTNESS (0–100) and SQUELCH (6–26). Short MDE or push toggles; push on SQUELCH, long MDE or a few seconds ends.
4.13 Warnings
  • LOW BATT, STUCK PTT, TX HOT, FAILURE appear in the bottom line every 5 seconds.
  • Below 10 V the speaker output switches off.
Installation Setup (installer)
  1. Hold MDE while switching on (keep the mouse button down on MDE, move over the volume knob and scroll up).
  2. PASSWORD: enter 6435 (turn = digit, push = next digit), short STO.
  3. ↕/SCN or push = next page, IC/SQL = previous page. Turn to change, STO to select / toggle / confirm. Values are stored at once.
  4. Switch off to leave.
